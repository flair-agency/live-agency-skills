# LIVE-characteristic tag policy

Choose a tag only when the latest feature observation contains direct support
and the characteristic is useful for evaluating or approaching the creator as
a LIVE creator. LIVE metrics may corroborate activity patterns but cannot alone
establish personality, interaction style, or content orientation.

Include durable traits such as an evidenced communication tendency, community
orientation, performance orientation, or specialist identity when—and only
when—the approved vocabulary contains the exact tag.

Exclude:

- short-video formats or genres such as VLog;
- `LIVE経験あり` or other facts directly readable from LIVE history;
- event participation, recruitment state, or another temporary condition;
- unsupported personality claims;
- sensitive or protected attributes; and
- a new synonym that is not in the approved vocabulary.

An empty tag list is valid. Prefer `insufficient_evidence` over a generic or
speculative assessment.
